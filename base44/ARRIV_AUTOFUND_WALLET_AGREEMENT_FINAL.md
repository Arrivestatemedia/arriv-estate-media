> This document is the final customer-facing wallet agreement prepared for counsel

## 1. What the wallet is

The Arriv Wallet holds **Booking Value** that a member can redeem against Arriv
services. It is not a bank account, it is not a deposit account, and it does not
bear interest.

## 2. Two kinds of Booking Value

| | Cash-funded value | Promotional value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where it comes from | The member's own wallet funding payments | Granted by Arriv, at no cash cost to the member |
| Redeemable against | Any Arriv service | Eligible services, and only up to the retail value of eligible services actually in the order |
| Cash value | Not cash, and no cash-out facility exists today | No cash value |
| VIP restriction | None | May not be applied to a standalone MLS Walkthrough |

Every lot records which kind it is, so promotional value can never be mistaken for
cash-funded value.

## 3. Lots and validity

1. Value is held in lots. Each lot carries the validity in force when it was issued — currently **12 months**.
2. Unused value is not re-issued, extended or reset at a billing-cycle boundary. The balance is the total of the lots that have not expired.
3. Arriv imposes no further expiry on cash-funded value beyond that lot validity, and asserts **no forfeiture rule**.
4. Whether the current validity period is lawful in every jurisdiction is **undetermined** and subject to counsel review.

## 4. Redemption

1. Cash-funded value may be redeemed against any Arriv service.
2. Promotional value is applied only up to the retail value of eligible services actually present in the order.
3. Redemption consumes the oldest value first.
4. Redemption does not create commissionable sales value: a wallet-funded order generates no booking-level sales commission, because the sales compensation for that funding was already settled when the member funded the wallet.

## 5. Rollover

Unused Booking Value does **not** roll into a new cycle as new value, and is not
extended or re-issued. It simply remains until its own lot validity ends. Nothing
in this agreement converts unused value into a new entitlement.

## 6. Cancellation

Cancelling the membership stops future recurring charges. It does **not** erase,
forfeit or confiscate cash-funded Booking Value: the balance stays in the wallet and
remains usable for Arriv services. Whether an unused balance must instead be
returned to the member is **undetermined** and is stated as undetermined.

## 7. Refunds of wallet value

Whether — and in what circumstances — an unused cash-funded balance must be
refunded rather than left as usable value is **undetermined** and subject to counsel
review. This agreement asserts neither that such a refund is required nor that it is
unavailable. Where applicable law grants a right, that right applies.

## 8. Dormant balances and unclaimed property

1. Arriv does not currently track dormancy, and asserts no dormancy rule.
2. Whether a dormant balance must be reported, transferred or treated in a particular way — including under any unclaimed-property regime — is **undetermined**.
3. Until counsel determines that question, Arriv asserts no right to take, reduce or forfeit a dormant balance.

## 9. Disputes and chargebacks

1. When a charge is disputed, Arriv suspends the unspent value that the disputed charge funded, per lot. Suspended value cannot be spent.
2. Suspension is not a redemption, an expiry or a fee. The lot keeps its remaining value, so a dispute resolved in the member's favour restores the exact amount.
3. A dispute resolved against the member reverses the suspended value.
4. Treatment of any already-redeemed portion is **undetermined** and subject to counsel review.
5. Arriv retains the evidence needed to respond to a dispute: the authorization and consent record, billing and transaction history, service delivery records and communications.

## 10. Errors and corrections

Duplicate, erroneous or unauthorized charges are corrected, and the corresponding
value is restored or removed so the wallet reflects what actually happened. Value
issued in error is not retained.

## 11. Cash-out

The wallet has **no cash-out facility** today. This is a statement of how the system
currently works, not a denial of any right the member may have under applicable law.

## 12. Records

Arriv keeps the transaction ledger for the wallet (funding, redemption, expiry,
dispute suspension and release, corrections), because a complete ledger is what
allows a balance to be reconstructed and a dispute to be evidenced.
