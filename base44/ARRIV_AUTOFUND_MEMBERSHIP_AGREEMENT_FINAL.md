## 1. The membership

The member enrolls in an Arriv Auto-Fund membership. Each membership month Arriv
adds a fixed amount of Booking Value to the member's Arriv Wallet and, where the
tier provides one, charges a monthly membership fee. The member authorizes the
**complete recurring charge** — wallet funding plus membership fee — before the
membership begins.

| Tier | Monthly wallet funding | Promotional bonus | Monthly membership fee | Total monthly recurring charge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $150 | 5% | $0 | $150 |
| Growth | $250 | 10% | $0 | $250 |
| Professional | $350 | 15% | $25 | $375 |
| Premier | $500 | 20% | $25 | $525 |
| VIP | $1,000 | 25% | $25 | $1,025 |

## 2. Recurring charges, authorization and renewal

1. The charge recurs monthly on the member's billing date.
2. Arriv discloses, and the member authorizes, the total recurring charge and its two components **before** the first charge.
3. Only the member may authorize the recurring charge. A salesperson or advisor cannot accept it on the member's behalf.
4. Arriv keeps a record of the authorization: the fee and funding amounts frozen at the time, the version of these terms, and the session evidence.
5. **Renewals are charged at the amount frozen in the member's accepted contract.** A later change to Arriv's published schedule does not silently re-price a live membership.
6. Arriv sends a renewal notice before the renewal charge, applying the advance-notice rule for the member's billing jurisdiction. The internal default is conservative; the statutory cadence for each state is subject to confirmation by counsel (see the counsel review package).

## 3. The membership fee is not wallet value

1. The membership fee is a **separate monthly charge for access to membership benefits**. It is not a deposit.
2. The fee buys no Booking Value. It is not spendable in the wallet, it creates no wallet balance, and it generates no promotional value.
3. The fee is not commissionable: no sales compensation is generated by it.

## 4. Promotional Booking Value

1. The tier's promotional bonus is credited as Booking Value at no cash cost to the member. The wallet records which value is promotional and which is cash-funded, so the two can never be confused.
2. Promotional Booking Value may be redeemed only up to the retail value of the eligible Arriv services actually present in the order. It does not create cash value and is not refundable as cash.
3. On the VIP tier, promotional Booking Value may not be applied to a standalone MLS Walkthrough.
4. Promotional value is subject to the same lot validity as the funding it accompanies (see §6).

## 5. Cash-funded wallet balances

1. Booking Value funded by the member's own payments is cash-funded value.
2. Cash-funded Booking Value may be redeemed against Arriv services.
3. Cancelling the membership does not erase, forfeit or confiscate cash-funded Booking Value. The balance remains in the wallet and remains usable.
4. Whether an unused cash-funded balance must instead be returned to the member on cancellation is **not yet determined** (see §8 and the counsel review package). Nothing in these terms asserts that the balance is non-refundable.

## 6. Validity of Booking Value (rollover)

1. Booking Value is held in lots. Each lot carries the validity in force when it was issued — currently **12 months**.
2. Unused Booking Value is **not re-issued, extended or reset** at a billing-cycle boundary. The wallet balance is the total of the lots that have not expired.
3. Arriv imposes no further expiry or forfeiture on cash-funded value beyond the lot validity in force. Whether that validity period is lawful in every jurisdiction is subject to counsel review.

## 7. Cancellation

1. The member may cancel at any time.
2. Cancellation stops future recurring charges. No further funding or membership fee is charged after the effective date.
3. Benefits end at the end of the paid membership period. Cash-funded Booking Value already in the wallet is preserved as described in §5.
4. Arriv confirms the cancellation to the member, stating the effective date, the end of the benefit period, and that the wallet balance is preserved.

## 8. Refunds

1. **Arriv's adopted policy:** a properly authorized membership fee is ordinarily nonrefundable once the membership period has begun and the benefits for that period are available.
2. **Refunds are always provided for:** a fee charged for a period that has not yet begun where the member cancels before it starts; duplicate, erroneous or unauthorized charges; charges made after an effective cancellation; and any circumstance in which Arriv materially fails to make the contracted membership benefits available, in which case the member receives an appropriate refund, adjustment or other legally required remedy.
3. **Applicable law overrides any conflicting provision of these terms.** Where the law gives the member a greater right, that right applies.
4. Refund rights in respect of an unused wallet balance (as distinct from the membership fee) remain undetermined and are stated as such rather than asserted.

## 9. Disputes and chargebacks

1. If a charge is disputed, Arriv suspends the unspent Booking Value that the disputed charge funded, per lot. Suspended value cannot be spent.
2. Suspension is not a redemption, an expiry or a fee: the lot keeps its remaining value, so if the dispute is resolved in the member's favour the exact amount is restored.
3. If the dispute is resolved against the member, the suspended value is reversed.
4. Arriv records the dispute, assembles the evidence (authorization and consent record, billing and transaction history, service delivery, communications) and alerts its team.
5. Treatment of any already-redeemed portion of a disputed charge is not yet determined and is subject to counsel review.

## 10. VIP enhanced support

> VIP members receive priority access to Arriv Assist, including automated assistance and escalation to a human support representative when necessary to resolve qualifying service-related issues.

1. This benefit adds priority and escalation. It does **not** promise a number of human support sessions, guaranteed human support hours, or a fixed number of complimentary consultations.
2. This benefit adds to, and reduces, no existing customer-support right that every Arriv customer already has.
3. Escalation is available when the request is a qualifying service-related issue. Automated assistance remains the normal first response.

## 11. Information Arriv keeps

Arriv keeps the member's accepted terms version, the frozen funding and fee amounts, the authorization evidence, and billing and notice history, as required for billing, dispute and audit purposes. Personal data captured as authorization evidence is retained for 24 months and then becomes eligible for deletion.

## 12. Support and notices

Notices are sent to the member's registered email address. Support is available through Arriv Assist and the published support channel.
